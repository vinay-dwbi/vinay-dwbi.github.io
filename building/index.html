hospitality venture · India

Idli Point & Chat Corner

A South Indian hospitality concept I took from idea through brand, operations, and a localized retail plan. Dine-in, delivery, catering, and a model that could stretch into franchising and cloud kitchens.

  • Brand identity and positioning for a regional concept with a clear product core.
  • Operational design: kitchen flow, service model, and day-to-day retail footprint.
  • Supply chain planning for perishable inventory and consistent product quality.
The expensive lesson: the hard problem was never the menu. It was supply, location, and the operating system of a small business. Everything I have built since starts from the constraint rather than the interface.
live in production · solo

SaaS & Automation Tooling

Custom workflow utilities, scraping pipelines, and site engines I design and operate myself. The flagship is Homemade, a hyperlocal marketplace connecting home cooks with people nearby.

  • Homemade: Next.js and TypeScript on Vercel, PostgreSQL on Supabase. Designed, built, secured, deployed, and operated alone. Running as a deliberate single-ZIP pilot.
  • RE Analyzer: ZIP-level housing inventory analysis. FastAPI and Python, with an embedded SQLite-compatible database at the edge. Runs for roughly a dollar a month.
  • Workflow utilities and automated site engines that turn repetitive operational work into a configuration problem.
Authorisation on Homemade lives in the database as row-level security rather than in the application layer. Not because it is more elegant, but because of the failure direction. A policy I forget to write denies access. An application check I forget to write leaks data.

Why I keep doing this

I have spent twenty years building platforms that serve someone else's product. That is real work and I am good at it. But there is a gap between reviewing a design and living with one, and the only way I know to close it is to ship something end to end and then run it for a while.

Everything on this page is small on purpose. None of it is where my ambition is. What it does is keep me honest about what production actually costs, so that when I tell a team their approach will not survive contact with real users, I am speaking from something more recent than memory.
