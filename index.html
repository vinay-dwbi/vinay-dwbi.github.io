Worth Beyond the Scoreboard…

September 26, 2026 · A 3-minute read

The classroom most of us sat in was designed for the factory. Bells marked the shifts. Desks stood in rows. Children moved through grades in batches, like parts along a line, and were graded on how well they produced the expected answer on time.

It was not a cruel design. It was a practical one. For most of history, the central problem was survival: food, shelter, a steady wage. A system that turned out reliable workers solved that problem for millions of families, including mine.

    What I believe

    1. Education should teach people how to live, not only how to earn.
    2. Self-worth that depends on someone else's approval can be taken away. Self-worth you earn from yourself cannot.
    3. Abundance is a responsibility. The systems that create it have to be trustworthy.
    4. Being wrong, and noticing it, is the fastest way to learn anything.
    5. Peace of mind is not the prize at the end. It is the way you walk.
    6. Solve a problem once, then give the answer away.
