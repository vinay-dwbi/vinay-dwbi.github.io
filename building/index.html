Founding Principal & Operational Strategist

A fast-food South Indian restaurant concept built to deliver authentic cuisine at accessible prices, scaled for dine-in, delivery, and mobile-first ordering.

  • Vision & strategy. A scalable quick-service model around South Indian staples — Tatte Idli, Medu Vada, Kesari Bath — designed for high throughput and fast order fulfillment.

  • Brand & footprint. End-to-end brand identity, packaging, menu engineering, and localized retail site strategy, with a path to franchising and cloud-kitchen expansion.
The core challenge of retail hospitality is never just the product. It is supply chain logistics, footprint economics, and operating efficiency under load.
